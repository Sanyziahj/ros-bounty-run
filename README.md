# ROS BOUNTY RUN: DAILY RIDER

A free daily arena roguelite in the browser: https://sanyziahj.github.io/ros-bounty-run/

A new bounty every day, the same for every rider. Pick Skippy (star baton), Jazz, Cavendish or the Hunter Rider, clear 5 rooms of VOVs and drones in Gutter Alley, the space yards and the casino, pick 1 of 3 upgrades after each room (synergies, plus the rare Santt's Call: Santt swoops in and blasts the field with his green raygun), then take down ROS, the bounty-giver. 3 tries a day, a daily streak with one grace day, a Bounty Archive to practice past days, an emoji share card, and a Certified Victor poster when you claim the daily bounty. Let's Ride!

Controls: keyboard (arrows/WASD move, K fire, SPACE dash, J special, L Santt, ENTER pause), gamepads (B fire, A dash, X special, Y Santt, START), and touch.
